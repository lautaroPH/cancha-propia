# Surface brief: Codeportistas web app (all routes)

Scope: whole web app — landing (Persuade), directory + athlete profile (Experience/Read), login + signup + dashboard + admin (Operate). Owner pinned the direction: Argentine identity, no flag clichés, restyle everything including admin.

Audience/job: athletes on phones creating a profile in minutes; supporters arriving from Instagram links deciding whether to help; a small admin team reviewing.

Constraints: no DNI/ID collection; Instagram via official per-post embeds only; never imply the platform handles money; no invented stats or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Codeportistas is an album de figuritas of Argentine athletes — every athlete is a collectible card you open to meet them. It refuses the crowdfunding template (headline, three icon cards, progress bars on grey).

OWN-WORLD: White ground; celeste #74ACDF owns whole regions (top band, hero field, figurita backgrounds); navy ink #0E1B33 for type and actions; sol #F6B40E reserved for support moments (campaign, alias). Archivo (Omnibus-Type, Buenos Aires) is the only family: display at 62% width, 800–900 weight, uppercase, tight leading; body at normal width. Figurita = photo on celeste inside a white frame with a navy name strip and a sport/province line. 4px corners, 1px hairlines, no floating shadow cards.

STORY: A supporter sees real athletes laid out as an album, opens one, reads their story and Instagram posts, and transfers directly. An athlete "arma su figurita": five fields, watching the card fill in, then finishes the rest from the dashboard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing — celeste field to the fold. Left: condensed navy headline, one line of support copy, a working search, and "Armá tu figurita" as the primary action. Right: a fanned stack of real published figuritas from the API (or a single labelled sample card when none exist). Signup — the form on the left, the live figurita preview on the right (stacked above the form on mobile).

FORM: Figurita album, pinned by the owner ("Argentine identity"); ordered list not rolled. seed: none (direction pinned by owner, concept-seed not run). Signature interaction: the live figurita preview that fills as the athlete types; directory figuritas tilt and lift slightly on hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
